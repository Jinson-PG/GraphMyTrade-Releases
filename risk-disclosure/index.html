TRADING RISK DISCLOSURE

Every trade carries risk.

Understand trading risks and the limits of analysis and historical results in GraphMyTrade.

Tools for review, not promises.

Trading stocks, options, futures, CFDs, forex, crypto assets and other products can result in significant or total loss of capital. Leverage and derivatives can magnify losses.

Past performance is not predictive

Reports, backtests, scores, historical observations and research outputs do not guarantee future profits or successful trades. Data may be incomplete or delayed, and market conditions change.

Broker data and automation

Where available, third-party connections depend on broker-specific API permissions, token validity, service availability and exchange or regulatory requirements. Risk checks cannot be assumed to close positions unless the exact behavior is separately documented and verified.

Independent decisions

GraphMyTrade does not offer individualized investment advice on this marketing site. Evaluate your own financial situation, use appropriate controls and seek professional advice where needed.
