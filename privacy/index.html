PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your trading data deserves care.

Read the GraphMyTrade privacy information and details that will be confirmed before the desktop software launches.

Pre-release policy draft. The final legal privacy notice must be reviewed and published before collecting customer information or payments.

Data you choose to provide

GraphMyTrade is designed around trade journaling and analysis. Information entered may include trade details, session context and journal notes. Optional broker connectivity requires user-provided credentials and the permissions supported by each provider.

Website information

This static marketing site does not intentionally collect trading records or broker credentials. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request logs. Google Analytics 4 is optional and only loads if a real measurement ID is configured and the visitor chooses "Allow analytics". The visitor may decline without losing site functionality; their analytics choice is remembered in local browser storage. Any reviews, payment providers and other services will be described in the final privacy notice before launch.

Desktop application and integrations

Actual data storage, retention, broker credentials, software updates and licensing behavior must be checked against the released desktop build before this notice becomes final.
